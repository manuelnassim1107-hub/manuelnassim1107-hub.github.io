Sveglia

An Android clock for your alarms, timers and routines.

Set repeating alarms, organise them into groups, choose a favourite sound, or set a bedtime reminder. World clocks, a stopwatch and timers are included.

Take your alarms to a new phone

In Clock settings, choose Google account and connect the account you want to use. Sveglia asks Google Drive for access to its own private app data so it can save your clock settings and restore them when you connect the same account on another phone.

Read the Sveglia privacy policy for the types of data saved and how to disconnect.
