Terms of service

These terms describe use of the Sveglia Android clock.

Using the clock

You choose the alarms, times, sounds and media on your device. Android manages alarm delivery and notifications according to the permissions and settings you select.

Optional Google sync

By connecting an account, you ask Sveglia to save and restore your clock data through that account's Google Drive. Choose an account you are authorised to use and media you have permission to store. Sync requires an internet connection. The Google account screen in the app displays the last confirmed sync time.

You can disconnect at any time. The privacy policy explains what is stored and how to remove it.
