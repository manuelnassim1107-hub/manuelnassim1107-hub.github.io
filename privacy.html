Sveglia

Privacy policy

Applies to Sveglia 1.2 for Android.

On your phone

Sveglia stores your alarms, labels, schedules, groups, preferences and other clock data on your device. You can use the clock offline. Connecting a Google account turns on optional sync.

When you connect Google Drive

Android lets you select a Google account. Sveglia keeps its account name on your device to identify the account to sync, and uses an access token supplied by Google to communicate directly with Google Drive over HTTPS. Google handles account authentication.

The Drive permission drive.appdata limits Sveglia to its own app data folder in your account. Sveglia uses this folder to save, merge and restore clock data across your devices. Synced content can include alarm times and labels, group names, selected sounds and alarm photos, world clocks, timer presets, stopwatch data and preferences. Selected audio and photos can include the actual media files.

Who receives data

Google receives the synced content to store it in the account you select. Sveglia uses Google account data for the sync you request. The app does not send your synced profile to a developer-operated server, sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

If you use city search or weather, Sveglia sends the city search text or selected city's coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve results. These requests are separate from Google account sync. If you share stopwatch results, Android sends the information to the destination you choose.

Disconnect or delete saved data

Choose Clock settings → Google account → Disconnect account to stop syncing that phone. Your local alarms and the copy already saved to Drive are kept. You can review or revoke Sveglia's access in your Google Account connections.

To remove the cloud copy, disconnect your synced devices first. In Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find Sveglia and use its option to delete hidden app data when available. To erase the local copy, use Android's app storage settings to clear Sveglia's data.

Privacy questions

You can contact the app publisher using the user support email shown in Sveglia's Google authorization details.
